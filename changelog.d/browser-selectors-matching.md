Browser engine: selector matching in `optimal-selectors`. Matches selector lists against elements through an `Element` trait (so any DOM can use it), and runs scoped queries the way `querySelectorAll()` does, with `:scope` and virtual scoping roots (a document or fragment). Covers type, universal, ID, class and attribute selectors with WHATWG HTML's case-sensitivity rules and quirks mode, all four combinators, `:is()`, `:where()`, `:not()`, `:has()`, the child-indexed and typed pseudo-classes (with `of S`), `:empty` (white space allowed, per the current spec), `:root`, `:lang()` (RFC 4647 extended filtering on well-formed tags), `:dir()`, the nesting selector `&`, and host-defined states through the trait. Complex selectors are matched without recursion on their length. Passes all 941 WPT dom/nodes querySelectorAll cases in scope (8 with errata) and agrees with a literal model on random trees and selectors. Shadow tree selectors match nothing until the DOM has shadow trees.
