- OptimalFS: a full volume no longer locks itself up. Deleting a big file on a full small volume and then creating one used to stop all writing until remount (issue 27), because getting freed space back takes up to 9 commits and each of those needs fresh space. The space check now keeps back what the next commit and that drain need, counting metadata in chunk slots, and works out whether a drain would succeed before writing anything. A refused operation on a full disk now writes nothing, and deletes keep working. Format now needs at least 1024 blocks (4 MiB). Measured data that fits in one file before the first refusal: 53% of a 4 MiB volume, 73% of 8 MiB, 82% of 16 MiB and 93% of 64 MiB; above about 256 MiB the 2% gap is what is kept back.
