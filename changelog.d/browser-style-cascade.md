Browser engine: the cascade (css-cascade-5 sections 5 and 6, with css-nesting-1 and css-namespaces-3) in a new crate, `optimal-style`. It collects the style rules of a document's sheets with their origin: nested rules (relative selectors made absolute, nested declarations rules), cascade layers (blocks, statements, anonymous and nested names, the layers of `@import`), `@namespace` and the placement rules for `@import` and `@namespace`, and `style` attributes. For each element it finds the declarations that apply, trying only the rules bucketed under the element's ID, classes and name, and sorts each property's declarations by origin and importance, element attachment, layer, specificity and order. Checked against a literal model on random sheets and trees, 111 runnable web-platform-tests cases, hand-worked spec examples and a scaling test. `optimal-selectors` gains `MatchSession::matches_nested`, so one session can match rules nested at different places. Not yet: loading `@import`ed sheets, `@media`, `@supports`, `@container` and `@scope` (dropped for now), property validity, defaulting and values.
