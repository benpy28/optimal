- Optimal Boot: BIOS stage, part 1. A new MBR boot sector and stage 2 (`bootloader/bios/`) boot SeaBIOS machines up to a verified, loaded kernel: A20, long mode, a real-mode thunk for BIOS calls, the E820 map, INT 13h extended reads, GPT and MBR boot volumes, and a read-only FAT12/16/32 reader with long names, then the same signed-config, SHA-512 and ELF checks as the UEFI loader. The handoff is part 2, so the stage stops with a named "cannot hand over yet" error and BIOS machines keep using Limine until then. New `optimal-boot-install bios` writes stage 1 and 2 without touching the partition table. New tested and fuzzed core parsers: CRC-32, MBR, GPT (with backup fallback), FAT and E820. New CI job `bios`: 25 SeaBIOS cases (GPT and MBR, FAT12/16/32, IDE, AHCI, virtio, NVMe, USB, SCSI, 128 MiB and 6 GiB, ten damage and tampering refusals) and 10 deliberate breaks, each caught.
