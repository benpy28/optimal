- OptimalFS stores all metadata twice, on single-disk volumes too. Metadata pointers hold a logical address, and a copy-on-write chunk table (whose root is in every one of the last 9 commits' root sets) maps each metadata chunk to two copies half the disk apart. The second copy is keyed differently, so a drive that deduplicates can't merge them, and both are checked against the one checksum in the parent. A bad copy is found by normal reads, recovery after a crash or a scrub, and is repaired at the next commit by moving it to a fresh place, never by writing over it. Chunks can be moved, and moved or freed metadata stays quarantined for 8 commits like everything else.
