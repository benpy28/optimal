Browser engine: new `optimal-selectors` crate, Selectors Level 4 parsing with the pseudo-elements of css-pseudo-4, the shadow tree selectors of css-shadow-1 and the nesting selector `&`. Parses selector lists and relative selector lists (from text or from a rule's prelude), resolves namespace prefixes, applies the validity rules (what may follow a pseudo-element, sub-pseudo-elements, no `:has()` in `:has()`, compound-only arguments, forgiving `:is()` and `:where()`), serializes per cssom-1 and computes specificity. Nesting deeper than 256 functional pseudo-classes is refused. Passes all 352 WPT css/selectors/parsing cases in scope (5 with errata where the 2026 spec text differs) and matches a separately written literal model on random selectors.
